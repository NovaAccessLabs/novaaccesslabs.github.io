NovaAccessScale

An app by NovaAccessLabs

NovaAccessScale turns a Bluetooth kitchen scale into a helper for blind and low-vision cooks. Tones and vibration guide you as you pour, and your own screen reader (TalkBack or VoiceOver) tells you the weight. You can set a target weight by voice, and the app tells you when you reach it.

  • Built for screen readers, with large high-contrast text.
  • No account, no internet, no tracking.
  • In English and French.
  • Android first, iPhone later. Coming soon.

Compatible scale

NovaAccessScale works with the KP2048B Bluetooth kitchen scale, which weighs up to 5 kg. The app finds the scale and connects to it on its own: there is no list of devices to pick from. Other scales are not supported yet.
