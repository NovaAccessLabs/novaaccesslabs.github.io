Privacy Policy — NovaAccessScale

Last updated: 22 June 2026

NovaAccessScale (“the app”) is developed by NovaAccessLabs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not request internet access and cannot send your data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

To function, the app saves the following only on your device, using your phone’s standard local storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your saved presets (a name and a target weight).
  • Your settings (feedback mode, spoken announcements, theme, high contrast, text size, language, auto-reconnect, demo mode).
  • The identifier and name of the last scale you connected to (so the app can reconnect automatically) and your last target weight.

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (scan and connect): used solely to discover and connect to your kitchen scale. The Bluetooth scan is declared “never for location,” and the app does not use, derive, or collect your location.
  • The app requests no internet permission.

Text-to-speech

Spoken announcements use your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The text is processed by your operating system on your device; it is not sent to us.

Third parties

We do not share data with any third party, because we do not collect any.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
