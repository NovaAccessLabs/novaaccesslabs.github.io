Privacy Policy — NovaAccessScale

Effective date: 25 October 2026

NovaAccessScale is an app for Android and iOS. It connects to a Bluetooth kitchen scale and guides you while you weigh, with sounds, vibration and your screen reader. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short:
  • The app collects no personal data.
  • It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not use the internet. On Android it doesn't even have the internet permission.
  • Everything happens on your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app saves four settings on your phone: the sound volume, the vibration setting, whether to keep the target after a pour, and whether you have finished the welcome screen. They never leave the app. If you turn on your phone's own backup (Google or Apple), your phone may include them in that backup, under your control and under your backup provider's terms.

Weights and targets are kept in memory only while the app runs. They are never saved and never sent anywhere.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to a compatible kitchen scale and to read its weight. It does not use Bluetooth to find your location. On Android it does not ask for the location permission. It saves no information about the scale or about other Bluetooth devices near you.

Microphone and voice commands

The microphone is used only when you start a voice command, for example to set a target weight. Your phone's own speech recognition turns your words into text on the device. The app requires on-device recognition and checks that it is available before every command: if it isn't, voice commands stay off rather than send your voice to an online service. The app uses the text once to run the command, then discards it. It records no audio and keeps no text.

Speech

Spoken messages go through your phone's screen reader (TalkBack or VoiceOver). The app has no speech engine of its own.

Links that leave the app

The About page and the settings contain links: an email to NovaAccessLabs, our Instagram page, and a page about compatible scales on novaaccesslabs.ch. They open only when you choose them, in your mail app, in Instagram or in your browser. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies. If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because the app collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. You remain free to contact us with any question. This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address with a new effective date.
